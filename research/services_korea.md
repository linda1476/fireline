# Commercial Services B: Korea + Wearables/Hardware Pain Points

Research date: 4 Nov 2026. Stream: Korean fire-service systems, recent LODD investigations, and global wearable/hardware pain points relevant to an AI voice-triage + clip-on display + bone-conduction concept.
Legend: **[V]** = checked against the cited source (often via news reporting of an official briefing). **[I]** = our inference. Quotes are under 15 words.

---

## 0. Thesis (one paragraph)

Korea has lost firefighters in three LODD incidents in under three years: Mungyeong (Jan 2024, 2 dead), Wando (Apr 2026, 2 dead) and Bucheon (Oct 2026, 1 dead). In every case the official finding or the documentary record says that **hazard or scene information did not reach, or did not change the actions of, the crews inside**. Mungyeong: edible oil. Wando: urethane foam. Bucheon: a crew member radioed that flammables were present, and an offensive strategy was announced four minutes later. The older precedent is the same. At Jecheon in 2017 (29 civilian deaths), the 119 situation room knew that "many people are on the 2nd floor" but passed it by mobile phone to a few officers and never broadcast it on the radio. The NFA's own fix after Wando (3 Jul 2026) is to upgrade the 119 상황관제시스템 so that **hazard and tactical information reaches dispatched crews in real time**. That is close to what our pillars 1 and 3 do. Korea already has the pipes: PS-LTE, the 119현장지원시스템 smartphone app, and AI call-intake pilots. What it lacks is a layer that **listens to the field, triages and fuses the information, and pushes it to the right responder**.

## 2. Firefighter LODD and injury statistics (sources conflict, so present as ranges)

- **Line-of-duty deaths in hazardous duty (위험직무순직), 2015–2024: 35.** Fire suppression was the largest category at 14, then rescue 6, everyday safety 5, aviation 5 and training 2. 2019 is the peak year because of a helicopter crash that killed 9 (some reports count 5). General line-of-duty deaths (cancer, suicide and other causes) were **86, about 2.5 times as many**. [fnnews 2026-10-23](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202610230630429018); [JKN](https://news.jkn.co.kr/post/1043150) **[V via search summaries]**
- **Approved on-duty injuries (공상)** have exceeded 1,000 a year for three straight years since 2022, up from about 409 in 2015 (NFA statistical yearbook). The union counts 1,447 in 2024. The 2024 yearbook snapshot shows 66,797 firefighters, 2 LODD and 1,334 공상. [KoreaScience PDF](https://www.koreascience.kr/article/JAKO202554650403375.pdf); [Hankyung 2024-02-07](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202402070719i) **[V; verify against 소방청 통계연보]**
- About 5 firefighter LODDs a year over 10 years; 44% had served 5 years or less. [Joongboo](https://www.joongboo.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=363523035) **[V headline]**
- Gyeonggi Province alone recorded **151 firefighter safety incidents** in 2024. [HelloDD 2025-12-19](https://www.hellodd.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=110313) **[V]**
- **PASS alarms (인명구조경보기):** 32,848 units nationwide on 1 Jan 2026, which is **49.8% of the 65,949 standard** based on all personnel. The NFA counters that this is **100.5%** of the 32,673 suppression crew who actually enter buildings. 17,388 units are two-way communication models. [MT 2026-10-21](https://www.mt.co.kr/policy/2026/10/21/2026102116113334431) **[V]**

## 3. Korean systems landscape

### 3.1 119 situation rooms (119종합상황실) and the next-generation 119 system
- **KT won the ISMP** (information-system master plan) for the 차세대 119통합시스템 in Mar 2026. Core elements: **AI speech-recognition call intake**, a nationwide integrated GIS, multimedia/video/IoT auto-reporting, and dispatch across jurisdiction borders. [Nate/KT 2026-03-18](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260318n28636) **[V]**
- **Daejeon** launched an AI-based intelligent 119 call-intake system in Oct 2023 with about **90.3% speech-recognition accuracy**. It later expanded to Seoul and Chungnam. [CCToday](https://www.cctoday.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=2225697) **[V]**
- **Gyeongnam** (articles dated around 3 Nov 2026) offers real-time two-way voice interpretation in **English, Japanese, Chinese and Vietnamese**. An **LLM extracts and summarises location, place and situation** for the dispatcher. ASR training data grew from 1,200 to 1,500 hours. [GN Maeil](https://www.gnmaeil.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=597905); [CWN](https://www.cwn.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=60889) **[V]**
- **Precise caller location** (Daejeon, Jun 2026, with MSIT) combines cell, Wi-Fi, BLE and barometer data to cut location error from about 30 m to about 15 m, with floor-level estimates. It is being considered for the next-gen 119 system. This locates **callers, not crew**. [Kyunghyang 2026-06-23](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202606231200001/); [Newspim](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260623000187) **[V]**
- **Gap [I]:** Every Korean AI deployment we found sits on the **inbound citizen-call side**. We found nothing that transcribes and triages **fireground radio traffic** for the incident commander. That is the white space for pillar 1.

### 3.2 Information to crews: 119현장지원시스템 and Seoul 소방안전지도
- The **119현장지원시스템** lets dispatched crews see hydrants, building drawings, on-site hazmat and responding units on smart devices without going through the 상황실. It was piloted in Gyeongnam in 2016 and **completed nationwide in Feb 2022**. [Safety1st](https://www.safety1stnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=2800); [FPN](https://www.fpn119.co.kr/177820) **[V]**
- Seoul runs a cloud-based next-generation 소방안전지도 (building information, vehicle availability, routes). [Seoul news](https://news.seoul.go.kr/safe/archives/516610) **[V]**
- **[I]:** The system existed before Mungyeong (2024) and Wando (2026), yet both investigations still cite hazard information not reaching crews. The bottleneck is **push, prioritisation and attention under load**, not whether a database exists. A phone app that must be opened with gloves on, in smoke, does not count as delivery.

### 3.3 Radio: 재난안전통신망 (PS-LTE), digital TRS and UHF
- **Itaewon (Oct 2022):** the Ministry of the Interior and Safety (행안부) admitted that agencies made poor use of the 재난안전통신망. Police used 1,536 terminals for 8,862 s, **fire used 123 terminals for 1,326 s**, and the medical side 11 terminals for 120 s. Seoul fire still ran legacy UHF that did not interoperate with police PS-LTE. [SEDaily](https://www.sedaily.com/article/13514243); [MBC](https://imnews.imbc.com/news/2022/politics/article/6423863_35666.html); [Weekly Kyunghyang](https://weekly.khan.co.kr/article/202211111506261) **[V]**
- **Cost:** total project budget of ₩1.4776 trillion through 2025; dedicated terminals have a **4-year service life**. [Newstapa](https://newstapa.org/article/QKQz5) **[V]**
- **Interoperability still unresolved in 2026:** after Gwangju and Jeonnam merged their fire services, Gwangju crews used mainly UHF and Jeonnam crews mainly PS-LTE. Gwangju's PS-LTE coverage was about 90%, so the problem was not a lack of devices but **which network crews actually use** during cross-border dispatch. [News1/Nate 2026-08-17](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260817n02918) **[V]**
- **Channel congestion:** Daejeon split fire and EMS onto separate radio channels in Jul 2026 to reduce cross-talk. [Seoul Shinmun 2026-07-31](https://www.seoul.co.kr/news/society/2026/07/31/20260731500114) **[V headline]**. A 2013 KIFSE paper describes multi-agency sharing of one frequency causing interference and making crew safety checks harder. [KIFSE 2013](https://www.kifsejournal.or.kr/upload/pdf/HJSBCY_2013_v27n1_60.pdf) **[V via summary]**
- **Field usability of the LTE radio:** a Goyang Ilsan firefighter's review (FPN, 4 Mar 2020) found that TRS/UHF audio arrived about **one beat late**, so transmissions often collided. Switching TRS to UHF took **about 8 touch steps**, including taking gloves off. He called it the worst inconvenience in a gloved fireground and asked for physical one-touch buttons. Some senior officers went back to analog radios. [FPN 2020-03-04](https://www.fpn119.co.kr/132299) **[V; one person's review, dated]**
- **2019 Gangwon wildfire** (four stations' after-action reports): units from other provinces could not talk to each other, and the national common channel was unusable because radio settings differed. The new **"commander-priority" talk function on digital radios was unstable**. There were relay dead zones in mountain valleys. Digital radio penetration was 96%. [SBS 2019-07-09](https://news.sbs.co.kr/news/endPage.do?news_id=N1005343223) **[V]**
- **2025 Yeongnam wildfire:** problems were attributed to agencies' familiarity with their own UHF radios rather than network performance. [ETNews 2025-04-03](https://www.etnews.com/20250403000277) **[V]**
- **Underground and inside large buildings:** the government says indoor and underground coverage relies on RAN-sharing with commercial networks. Gyeonggi fire funded a mobile backhaul base-station R&D in 2022 because deep underground spaces are hard to communicate in. [MOIS](https://mois.go.kr/frt/sub/a06/b11/policyBriefingView/screen.do); [NewsSeoul](https://newsseoul.co.kr/news/view/179588459678322) **[V]**. LG U+ gives fire-service SIMs **priority access** on its LTE network (Jul 2026). [Nate 2026-07-19](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260719n11941) **[V]**
- **Not found:** a 국정감사 or 감사원 finding that quantifies **indoor PS-LTE failure** for fire. The Board of Audit's Oct 2025 "disaster and safety management system" audit covered Itaewon, the Miryang hospital fire and the East Coast wildfire, and concluded that **infrastructure was over-invested relative to people**. It did not specifically address the radio network. [Daum/Oct 2025](https://v.daum.net/v/20251024175447821?f=p) **[V]**

### 3.4 Crew location tracking (소방관 위치추적)
- The **NFA's "infrastructure-less firefighter location estimation and control system"** runs 2026–2027 under the field-technology R&D programme. Siters leads, with KAIST, VI Soft and Tover; field testing is at Daejeon fire HQ. It uses GNSS, IMU and barometer data fused with AI, recognises landmarks such as stairs and lifts, and detects floor changes and falls. Terminals connect in a **mesh network over 900 MHz Zigbee or LoRa (600 m)**, and the control room shows positions on an **offline 3D map built from BIM/CAD drawings and LiDAR**. Targets: **2.5 m horizontal and 1 m vertical**, under 500 g, IP67, 90 minutes of heat resistance. Early tests reached about 1 m. [HelloDD 2025-12-19](https://www.hellodd.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=110313); [ZDNet 2025-12-19](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20251219140232) (headline: building-interior 3D modelling is essential) **[V]**
- Daejeon Seobu fire station, KAIST and a private firm signed an MOU in Jun 2026 for similar crew positioning without GPS. [Newspim 2026-06-09](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20260609000995) **[V]**
- An earlier ETRI-era report: "Firefighter location tracking using the PoLaS technique" (2015). [KISTI ScienceON](https://scienceon.kisti.re.kr/srch/selectPORSrchReport.do?cn=TRKO201500015581) **[V title]**
- **Status [V]:** no national roll-out of crew indoor tracking. It is still R&D until 2027.

### 3.5 Smart helmets, HUDs and thermal cameras
- The **National Fire Research Institute (국립소방연구원)** built a lightweight smoke-penetrating thermal camera with KERI, using LWIR with FPGA smoke removal. Volume fell 66% and weight 64% (1,280 g to 456 g). It is IP67 and **operable with thick firefighting gloves**. It is being piloted at 4 fire stations until **27 Nov 2026**. It is **handheld, not helmet-mounted**. [Newspim 2026-10-27](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20261027000803); [DJournal](https://www.djournal.co.kr/news/article.html?no=132624) **[V]**
- An older national R&D report, "Smart helmet for firefighters" (around 2019), covered a thermal display, IP55 and drone linkage. [DOI TRKO201900014305](https://data.doi.or.kr/10.23000/TRKO201900014305) **[V title/summary]**
- **No fielded Korean HUD helmet was found [V-negative].** The Wando investigation found that even the existing thermal cameras were **poorly managed**. [Etoday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2600005)
- **Heat-illness wearables:** the 국립소방연구원 plans ₩500M over 3 years for heart-rate, ECG and core-temperature sensors with early warning. A first trial with about 30 firefighters ran at the Gyeonggi fire academy in Sep 2026. [fnnews 2026-09-15](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609151245257643); [Safety News](https://www.safetynews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=250574) **[V]**
- The vital-sign tracking devices promised after Pyeongtaek: **no deployment figures found** [V-negative].

## 4. Global wearable and hardware pain points (relevant to a clip-on display + bone-conduction earpiece)

- **Heat kills electronics, especially the exposed parts.** NIST (2006) found that **all 7 portable radios failed within 15 minutes at 160 °C (Thermal Class II)**, and 3 did not recover after cooling. Radios protected in a pocket survived, but **exposed cords, speakers and microphones failed** at Class III (260 °C for 5 min). [ScienceDaily 2006](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2006/09/060930094159.htm); [Urgent Comms](https://urgentcomm.com/land-mobile-radio/cranking-up-the-heat-on-firefighters-radios) **[V]**. *[I] A clip-on display or an earpiece cable is exactly that kind of exposed component.*
- **Talking through an SCBA facepiece is poor.** Patent literature describes speech-diaphragm intelligibility as "generally poor". [USPTO 5404577](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5404577) **[V, patent claim]**. Mungyeong officials said radios are hard to use with gloves and SCBA on. [korea.kr](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=156619810&pWise=sub&pWiseSub=C4)
- **Bone-conduction evidence is promising but not proven for firefighting.** In a full-face respirator study using the Modified Rhyme Test, a bone-conduction earpiece outperformed the fielded air-conduction system. [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/266510533_Bone_conduction_systems_for_full-face_respirators_Speech_intelligibility_analysis). Military field use was rated well, with a small sample. [Letowski, Patriot 2007](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Tomasz-Letowski/publication/277756870_User_evaluation_of_a_bone_conduction_communication_headset_during_the_Patriot_2007_Joint_Field_Training_Exercise/links/570d0c7408ae3199889b4958/User-evaluation-of-a-bone-conduction-communication-headset-during-the-Patriot-2007-Joint-Field-Training-Exercise.pdf). NASA notes that the contact pressure bone conduction needs can make it **uncomfortable for extended wear**. [NASA NTRS 2024](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20240005885/downloads/ICES_final_manuscript_Cavanaugh.pdf) **[V]**. **We found no study of bone conduction with a firefighter SCBA under fireground noise.** Korean searches also found none (2024 KIFSE work on SCBA satisfaction is unrelated). [Kyobo](https://scholar.kyobobook.co.kr/article/detail/4010070347367)
- **Gloves defeat touch screens.** See the FPN LTE-radio review above. The NFR thermal camera made glove-operability a headline feature. [FPN](https://www.fpn119.co.kr/132299); [Newspim](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20261027000803) **[V]**
- **HUD and in-mask displays carry human-factors risk.** A Fire Engineering instructor says HUD air gauges add psychological stress that can change how firefighters regulate their air use, and that reliance on thermal cameras eroded basic search skills. [Fire Engineering / Steedman](https://www.fireengineering.com/firefighting-equipment/trevor-steedman-scba-boot-camp-a-firefighter-s-survival-school/) **[V summary]**. The only academic in-mask display prototype we found is small (corner display, NASA-TLX). [arXiv 2308.13021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.13021) **[V]**. Vendors claim their HUDs sit below the line of sight and do not distract (Honeywell TITAN; vendor claim). [Fire Apparatus](https://www.fireapparatusmagazine.com/firefighter-ppe/scba/scba-technology-gives-firefighters-more-information-greater-situational-awareness-greater-protection/)
- **What crews reject:** surveillance, and loss of autonomy or job status. In a 2025 qualitative study, rank-and-file firefighters **opposed wearables on calls while leadership supported them**, citing privacy, autonomy and identity. [PMC12673935](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12673935/) **[V]**. An Austrian survey found perceived compatibility and complexity to be acceptance barriers. [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396376756_Explaining_firefighters'_technology_acceptance_Findings_from_an_Austrian_cross-sectional_study) **[V]**. PPE makers report that the technology is immature. [Springer 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40691-026-00469-8) **[V]**
- **Comms recur as a factor in US LODDs.** NIOSH reports include maydays that nobody acknowledged (Connecticut 2014), garbled radio traffic in and out of the structure (North Carolina 2008), and a recommendation that dispatch monitor and record all fireground traffic (Pennsylvania 2021). [CDC FACE 2021-12](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/firefighters/programs/pdfs/face202112RS.pdf); [Firefighter Nation (CT)](https://www.firefighternation.com/?p=31072); [Firehouse (NC)](https://www.firehouse.com/home/news/10473449/niosh-releases-north-carolina-lodd-report) **[V]**. We found no rigorous percentage figure.

## 6. Implications for our product

### Pain points we can credibly attack
1. **Hazard information is lost between the 상황실 and the entry crew** (Mungyeong oil, Wando urethane, Bucheon flammables). Build: when a hazard keyword is spoken or a building record matches, push an audio and display brief to every responder assigned to that sector. muse-voice-transcribe can use keyword biasing for terms such as 우레탄, 인화물질 and 식용유.
2. **Radio reports that never change command decisions** (Bucheon: flammables reported at 08:48, offensive strategy announced at 08:52). Build: triage the radio into **Danger** cards that the IC must acknowledge, with an audit trail.
3. **Victim locations reported but not broadcast** (Jecheon: "2층 사람 많다" passed by phone only). Build: tag calls and transcripts as **Victim Found/Reported**, pin them to the floor plan, and send them to the rescue crew's earpiece.
4. **Radio silence nobody notices** (Bucheon about 23 minutes; Pyeongtaek last contact around 09:30; NIOSH unacknowledged maydays). Build: a per-responder "last heard" timer in the world model that escalates to the IC when a crew inside goes quiet.
5. **Glove and SCBA usability** (official Mungyeong admission; 8 touch steps on the LTE radio). Build: one-touch, large-target or hardware-button interactions, with audio as the primary channel and the display as secondary.
6. **Fragmented networks** (UHF vs PS-LTE, inter-provincial channels). Position the product as a **network-agnostic layer above the radio**: it transcribes whatever audio the command post receives. Do not pitch it as a radio replacement.
7. **Crew location is unknown** (national R&D runs to 2027). Our world model can **fuse spoken position reports** ("3층 동측 계단") and touch marks today, and accept a positioning feed later.

### Hardware claims to avoid
- **Do not claim** that bone conduction is intelligible under SCBA in fireground noise. No firefighter study exists. Say "simulated earpiece; audio-first design."
- **Do not claim** that a HUD or clip-on display reduces cognitive load or improves survival. The evidence is thin and practitioners see risks. Frame the display as glanceable and optional.
- **Do not claim** any indoor localisation accuracy. 2.5 m / 1 m is a 2027 R&D target, not a product, and our locations are *estimated* from voice and touch.
- **Do not claim** heat or IP ratings. NIST showed that exposed mics and cords fail first, and our hardware is simulated.
- **Do not claim** that "PS-LTE doesn't work indoors." The documented problems are interoperability, latency, usability and under-use. Pitch the product as working on top of any radio audio.
- **Avoid surveillance framing.** Rank-and-file firefighters resist being tracked. Present the product as "your crew gets the intel" and "the IC never misses your mayday", not as monitoring.
- **Avoid medical claims.** Vital-sign or heat-illness prediction moves toward medical advice, which the hackathon disqualifies.
