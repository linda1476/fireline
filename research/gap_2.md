## (b) Recommended slide statistic (ONE line)

**EN:** "Over a third of firefighter maydays (35.6%) go unheard on the first call, across more than 9,000 recorded maydays (Project Mayday, 2021). Communication is a contributing factor in about 1 in 5 near-miss reports (NFFNMRS, n=4,814)."

**KO:** "소방관 메이데이 3건 중 1건 이상(35.6%)이 첫 호출에서 응답받지 못합니다(Project Mayday, 9,000건 이상 녹음 분석, 2021). 아차사고 보고 5건 중 1건은 '통신'이 원인으로 지목됩니다(NFFNMRS, 4,814건)."

Optional third line for the same slide, as a case study: *Bridgeport 2010 (NIOSH F2010-18): the incident commander juggled two radio channels on two handhelds, a mayday went unacknowledged, and 2 firefighters died. NIOSH Recommendation #1: monitor radio transmissions and act on them quickly.*

Optional Korea line: *Mungyeong 2024 (소방청 합동조사, 2024.3.13): "현장 활동 정보 공유가 미흡" (on-scene information sharing was inadequate), 2 firefighters killed.*

## (c) Numbers we must NOT use (unverified or misattributed)

- **"54% of maydays missed on the first call."** No primary source found. The only Project Mayday "54%" we found is a different statistic: *54% of maydays involve a member of the first-arriving crew* (~1,000 maydays, 13 Apr 2016, https://www.firefighterclosecalls.com/?p=3236). The "54% missed" claim is most likely a conflation. Drop it.
- **"36.5% missed."** This figure appeared only in a search-engine summary. The Fire Engineering 2021 page itself says 35.6%. Use 35.6% (or "36%" rounded).
- **USFA EFO "82.6%".** No source found in any USFA document. Drop it.
- **A "% of NIOSH LODDs caused by communications" figure.** No primary source quantifies it. Kunadharaju et al. 2011 (189 investigations) and Hard et al. 2019 give categories and ranks, not a communications %. Do not invent one. Say "a top-5 recommendation category in NIOSH investigations" instead.
- **"~3,000 / 5,000 recordings" as the Project Mayday denominator.** These are inconsistent snippet figures tied to other sub-analyses. Use "more than 9,000 maydays" (2021).
- **The "79 seconds" acknowledgement time** may be used only with the caveat "reported by Firehouse, 2019". It has no denominator and Abbott's own write-ups do not repeat it.
- **Any Korean "% of LODD or injuries due to radio/communication failure."** The NFA yearbook and data.go.kr breakdowns are by activity (화재/구조/구급/생활안전/기타), with **no 통신/무전 category**. No KIFSE or 국립소방연구원 paper with such a % was found.
- **Pyeongtaek "radio lost at 09:30" as an NFA finding.** It is press-reported only. Present it as "reported" or cite the press.

## Reconciliation note (36 vs 35.6 vs 54)
- 35.6% is Don Abbott's own figure, published in Fire Engineering in Jun 2020 (>6,438 maydays, through end-2019) and again in Jul 2021 (>9,000 maydays). The figure stayed the same while the sample grew.
- 36% is the same number rounded, in Goldfeder's Firehouse column (Feb 2019).
- 54% is a different metric (the first-arriving crew's share of maydays, 2016). It is not a miss rate.

## Implications for our product
- **Lead the problem slide with 35.6% missed maydays (Project Mayday, >9,000 maydays).** It is the most defensible, large-n number and maps directly onto Pillar 1 (voice triage flags "Mayday" and "Danger" so nothing is missed).
- **Row 3 is our design argument.** Miss rates fall from 26% to under 1.8% when the IC has a quieter channel or a headset. An AI listener that never "steps outside" gives the IC the same advantage in software.
- **Bridgeport (two channels, two handhelds) is the demo scenario.** It is exactly the multi-channel, multi-speaker load that `muse-voice-transcribe` with diarization plus `muse-spark` priority tagging is meant to absorb. Script the demo on that pattern: a mayday arrives on channel B while the IC is transmitting on A, and the system pins it.
- **Make "unacknowledged mayday" a first-class state.** Escalate if there is no acknowledgement within N seconds (79 s is the reported average). This is a concrete agentic behaviour for the Best Agentic Use-Case prize.
- **The near-miss 1-in-5 figure** supports the claim that communication failures are common, not rare. Pair it with Mungyeong's official "정보 공유 미흡" finding to make the Korean case.
- **Korea framing:** Korean statistics have no radio or communication category. Present this as a gap our system helps close: logging and tagging radio traffic creates the data that does not exist today. Do not claim a Korean percentage.
- **Keep the tone safety and operations.** This is coordination tooling and operational logging, not medical advice, which keeps us clear of the anti-project list.
- **Footnote every number on the slide** with its year and n. Judges will spot inconsistent stats (36 / 35.6 / 54), so use 35.6% everywhere.
